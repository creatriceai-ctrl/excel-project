## Bike Purchase Analysis 'Excel Sales Dashboard'

An interactive Excel sales dashboard created to analyze customer purchasing behavior for bicycles based on income levels, demographic categories, commute distances, and geographic regions.

##Project Overview

This project cleans, models, and visualizes sales data to determine key customer trends and identify which demographics are most likely to purchase a bike.


##Key Findings & Insights

### 1.Average Income per Purchase (AVG Income per Purchase)
* Gender & Income: Customers with higher average income levels are more likely to purchase a bike compared to those who do not.
* Male vs. Female: Male customers show slightly higher overall income averages and a higher purchase rate compared to female customers.

### 2.Customer Age Brackets
* Middle Age Dominance: The vast majority of buyers (86%) belong to the Middle Age group.
* Adolescents: Make up a smaller share (14%) of overall purchases.

### 3.Customer Commute Distance
* Short Commutes (0–1 Miles): Customers residing closer to their workplace show the highest purchase volume.
* Longer Commutes: Bike purchases drop significantly as commute distance increases (e.g., 10+ miles), though moderate activity is visible in the 2–5 mile range.

## Interactive Filters (Slicers)

The dashboard allows users to dynamically filter data by:

* Education Level: Bachelors, Graduate Degree, High School, Partial College, Partial High School
